Break and defend an AI assistant

Start with an ordinary supplier invoice. See how the work gets done, then introduce an altered invoice and inspect what changes. Every send is simulated.

Explain the normal business workflow

Invoice arrives by email or a supplier portal. Accounts Payable checks the supplier, purchase order, receipt of goods and amount, then records it and routes it for approval. A person can do these steps manually, or an assistant can help extract details and draft or send an internal confirmation. Payment requires its own approval.

This lab demonstrates only the assistant reading an invoice and sending a processing confirmation. It does not simulate the inbox, purchase-order checks, approval or payment.

Choose a step, then press Send. A model refusal is not evidence that the code blocked an attempt.

After the attack: choose your session theme

Fix the assistant: identify where invoice content became an instruction, inspect the proposed send, and put the permission decision in tool code.

Explore security roles: Governance & Risk defines permitted data use, owners and evidence; Engineering enforces tool permissions and tests; IT/Infosec manages identities, access, monitoring and incident response. These responsibilities overlap and teams vary.

Then demonstrate one protection and verify that normal work still succeeds.
